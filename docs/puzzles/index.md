## 💡 Problem-Solving Framework

### **1. Understand the Problem**

- Read carefully and identify all constraints

- Clarify any ambiguities

- Restate the problem in your own words

### **2. Break Down the Problem**

- Identify what you know

- Identify what you need to find

- Look for patterns or similar problems

### **3. Start with Simple Cases**

- Try with smaller numbers

- Work through examples manually

- Look for patterns

### **4. Think Systematically**

- Use logical reasoning step-by-step

- Consider all possibilities

- Eliminate impossible cases

### **5. Optimize**

- Look for the most efficient solution

- Consider edge cases

- Verify your solution works for all cases

### **6. Communicate Clearly**

- Explain your thought process

- Walk through your reasoning

- Be ready to discuss alternatives

---

## 🔑 Key Techniques

### **For Logic Puzzles:**

- **Systematic elimination:** Rule out impossible cases

- **Working backwards:** Start from the solution

- **Case analysis:** Consider all possible scenarios

- **Constraint satisfaction:** Use given constraints effectively

### **For Optimization Puzzles:**

- **Binary search thinking:** Divide and conquer

- **Dynamic programming:** Build up from smaller cases

- **Greedy algorithms:** Make locally optimal choices

- **Mathematical analysis:** Use formulas and patterns

### **For Probability Puzzles:**

- **Conditional probability:** Use Bayes' theorem

- **Expected value:** Calculate averages

- **Symmetry arguments:** Use symmetry to simplify

- **Simulation:** Think through scenarios

### **For Game Theory:**

- **Backward induction:** Work from the end

- **Nash equilibrium:** Find stable strategies

- **Minimax thinking:** Consider opponent's best response

## 📖 Study Tips

1. **Practice Daily:** Solve 2-3 puzzles per day consistently

2. **Think Aloud:** Practice explaining your reasoning

3. **Time Yourself:** Aim for 15-30 minutes per puzzle

4. **Review Solutions:** Understand multiple approaches

5. **Group Similar Puzzles:** Identify patterns across problems

6. **Mock Interviews:** Practice with a friend or mentor

7. **Don't Memorize:** Focus on understanding the logic

---

## 🎯 Interview Tips

### **During the Interview:**

1. **Ask clarifying questions** - Don't assume constraints

2. **Think out loud** - Show your reasoning process

3. **Start simple** - Begin with small cases

4. **Be systematic** - Show organized thinking

5. **Verify your answer** - Check edge cases

6. **Stay calm** - Take your time, don't rush

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- Jumping to conclusions too quickly

- Not considering all constraints

- Forgetting edge cases

- Not explaining your thought process

- Giving up too early
