## 📖 How to Use This Repository

1. **Practice Systematically:** Start with easier puzzles (⭐⭐) and progress to harder ones

2. **Understand the Logic:** Focus on understanding the reasoning, not memorizing solutions

3. **Think Aloud:** Practice explaining your thought process clearly

4. **Time Yourself:** Practice solving within 15-30 minutes (typical interview time)

5. **Review Solutions:** Understand multiple approaches and optimizations

6. **Mock Interviews:** Practice explaining your approach step-by-step
