# Platform Engineering

> **Reviewed:** 2026-09 · **Level:** Senior / Tech Lead · **Type:** Emerging

Platform engineering asks: how do we let many product teams ship safely without each one becoming Kubernetes, Terraform and security experts? It's an emerging area, so interviewers value clear reasoning about trade-offs over tool names.

## Pages

- [Platform Engineering & GitOps](./01-platform-engineering-and-gitops.md) — 7 questions: what platform engineering is, golden paths, internal developer platforms, GitOps pull reconciliation (with Argo CD example), self-service with guardrails, measuring adoption, AI in platform workflows.

## What interviewers look for

- Treating the platform as a product with developers as customers.
- Golden paths that are optional but clearly the easiest route.
- GitOps benefits (audit, drift correction, no cluster credentials in CI) and its pitfalls.
