# Cloud

> **Reviewed:** 2026-09 · **Level:** Senior / Tech Lead · **Type:** Foundational

Senior interviews rarely test certification trivia. They test whether you can pick sensible building blocks, lay out a secure network, plan for failure, and keep an eye on the bill.

## Pages

- [AWS Cloud Architecture](./01-aws-cloud-architecture.md) — 23 questions (Q95–Q117): EC2 vs Lambda, storage, databases, messaging, networking and scaling on AWS.
- [Multi-Cloud, Networking & Cost](./02-multi-cloud-networking-and-cost.md) — 9 questions: AWS/GCP/Azure equivalents, IAM models, when multi-cloud is worth it, VPC design, firewalls, HA and DR, choosing compute, cost drivers, shared responsibility.

## What interviewers look for

- You reason in categories and trade-offs, not product names.
- Private-by-default networking and least-privilege identity.
- RTO/RPO-driven availability design and cost awareness.
